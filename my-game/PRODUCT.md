# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: people meeting 김호성 for the first time (club, gathering, class). They open a shared link, most likely on a phone, and spend a minute getting a feel for who he is.

## Product Purpose
Personal introduction pages. `index.html` is the sincere self-introduction (호성고치 virtual-pet design: who I am, what I like); `index2.html` is the humor page about the nickname 걸음마 (걸음의 악마). Success: a new acquaintance remembers the name, the two hobbies, and leaves with a smile.

## Operating Context
Static files opened directly in a browser (`index.html`, `index2.html`); shared as a link. No build step, no server.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Plain static HTML/CSS/JS, single file per page.
- Five clickable egg pixel characters, one per line of dialogue, randomly placed across sections on each load. Lines for index.html: '반가웡!', '안녕하세요!', '어서 오세요!', '세상에서 가장 가난한 왕은?', '최저임금'. The joke question must always appear above (before) its answer on the page.
- Egg character pixel design (10×12 cream egg, dot eyes, blush) is fixed and must be preserved.
- index.html section order is fixed: 소개 → 노래 감상 → 장거리 걷기 → 오늘의 TMI → 마무리.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: 김호성. Nickname: 걸음마 (short for 걸음의 악마).
- Hobbies: 장거리 걷기, 노래 감상.
- index.html is sincere introduction first; humor stays light (the egg lines carry it).

## Evidence on Hand
- TMI: lunch on 2026-10-06 was 스팸 김밥 and 닭강정.
- No specifics yet on favorite songs/artists or walking routes; do not invent them.

## Product Principles
- Introduce the person, not the website: name and hobbies are legible within seconds.
- Cute, never childish or cluttered.
- Pixel character, readable text: body copy is never set in a pixel font.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Korean body text must stay comfortably readable on phones; respect reduced motion.
